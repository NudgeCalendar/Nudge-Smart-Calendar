Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

The short version

  • Your calendar, contacts and location stay on your devices. Nudge has no accounts and no servers that store your data.
  • What you type into Nudge's assistant is understood on your iPhone. It doesn't leave the device.
  • Your settings, rules and places sync through your own private iCloud, which we can't see.
  • No tracking, no ads, no analytics tools, and we never sell data.

Who we are

Nudge is made by an independent developer in the United Kingdom. If you have any questions about this policy, email nudgecalendar@gmail.com.

What Nudge uses on your device

Nudge only uses the things below if you allow it. You can change any of them at any time in the Settings app, and see their status in Nudge under Settings › Permissions.

  • Calendars. To show your events, work out when to leave, and add or change events when you ask. Nudge uses the calendars on your device (such as iCloud, Google, Outlook or Exchange). Calendar rules you create only change how events look in Nudge, not your calendar.
  • Location. To time journeys from where you are. If you choose "Always" and turn on "Use location in the background", Nudge also re-checks leave times as you move and notices when you've arrived, so alerts stop. Your location stays on your device, except for the map lookups described below.
  • Contacts. Optional. With "Find people in event names" on, events like "Lunch with Sam" are linked to Sam's contact card so you can call or message them. This matching happens on your device.
  • Microphone and speech recognition. Optional, only when you tap the mic in the search bar, to turn what you say into text. Where your iPhone supports it, this happens on the device. Otherwise Apple's speech recognition service is used, under Apple's privacy policy.
  • Notifications. For time to leave alerts, heads-ups, reminders, the morning summary and weather alerts. These are scheduled on your device.
  • Alarms. Optional. If you turn on the wake-up alarm, Nudge sets an alarm on your device from your first leave time.
  • Siri, Shortcuts and Spotlight. Nudge makes your upcoming events available to Siri and Spotlight on your device, so you can ask things like "When do I leave?". This uses Apple's on-device systems.

The assistant

When you type or say an event, change or question in the search bar, Nudge works out what you mean on your iPhone, using Apple Intelligence where available or Nudge's built-in understanding. What you type isn't sent to us or to anyone else.

Services Nudge uses

To give you leave times, routes and forecasts, Nudge sends only what's needed to Apple's services. It doesn't send your name or the details of your events.

  • Apple Maps (MapKit). Start and end locations, to calculate routes and travel times by foot, bike, public transport and car, to search for places, and to find nearby car parks and bike hire.
  • Apple Weather (WeatherKit). A location and time, to show the forecast for your events and journeys.
  • iCloud. If iCloud sync is on, your Nudge settings, calendar rules, event types and places are stored in your private iCloud account so they're the same on all your devices. Only you can see this data. Your events themselves are synced by your calendar provider, not by Nudge.

These services are covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Links that open other websites

  • Flight tracking. If you turn on flight tracking and open a flight, Nudge opens its live status on the Flightradar24 website. That website receives the flight number. See Flightradar24's privacy policy.
  • Meeting links. Join buttons open the meeting link in its own app or website, such as Zoom, Google Meet or Microsoft Teams.
  • Directions. Directions open in Apple Maps.

Coming soon list

The "Coming soon" page in Settings downloads a short public list of planned features from GitHub. No personal information is sent, though, like any website, GitHub can see the request came from your internet connection.

Purchases

Nudge Pro subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. Apple only tells the app whether your subscription is active, so Pro features can be unlocked. You can manage or cancel your subscription in your Apple Account settings.

Analytics and crash reports

Nudge doesn't use any analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and doesn't track you across other apps or websites. If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone settings, Apple may give us anonymous crash reports and usage statistics, which we use only to fix problems.

Emails you send us

If you email us to report a bug or request a feature, we receive your email address and what you write, plus the app version and device details Nudge adds to the message so we can help. We use these only to reply and improve Nudge, and delete them when they're no longer needed.

Data we keep

Nudge doesn't have servers that store your personal data, so apart from emails you send us, there's nothing about you on our side to keep or delete.

Children

Nudge isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law (UK GDPR), you have rights over your personal data, including to access it, correct it and ask for it to be deleted. Because Nudge keeps your information on your devices and in your own iCloud, you can delete it at any time by deleting the app and its iCloud data (Settings › your name › iCloud). For any question or request, email nudgecalendar@gmail.com. You can also contact the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Nudge uses information, we'll update this page and the date at the top. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.
