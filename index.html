Never leave late again.

Nudge reads your calendar and tells you exactly when to leave, with live traffic, public transport and weather added in.

Coming soon to the App Store iPhone, Apple Watch and Mac

Know when to leave

Every event with a place counts down to the minute you need to go, by foot, bike, public transport or car. Then Nudge tells you, even through Focus.

Arrive the way you like

Set how early to arrive for each type of event, for words like "interview", and for flights and trains. Extra time is added when rain is likely.

Type it like a text

"Dinner with Sam Friday 7pm" becomes an event. "Push the dentist back 30 min" moves it. Or just say it.

Works with your calendars

Uses the iCloud, Google, Outlook and Exchange calendars already on your iPhone. Rules fix events your calendar gets wrong.

The people you're meeting

"Lunch with Callum" links to Callum's contact card, so you can call or message if you're running late.

On every screen

A live countdown on your Lock Screen and Dynamic Island, widgets, Apple Watch, Mac, Control Center and Siri.

Leave times and alerts are always free
