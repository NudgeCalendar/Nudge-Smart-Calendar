Common questions

How is my leave time worked out?

Nudge takes the event's start time and subtracts the journey from where you'll be (your previous event, where you are, or Home), live traffic for driving, extra time if rain is likely and you're walking or cycling, and how early you like to arrive. You set how early in Settings › Arrive early, by type of event, for words like "interview", and for flights and trains. Anything you change on a single event always wins.

My events aren't showing

Check Nudge has Full Access to Calendars in the Settings app › Apps › Nudge › Calendars, and that the calendar is switched on in Nudge under Settings › Calendars. Google, Outlook and other calendars need to be added to your iPhone first, in the Settings app › Apps › Calendar › Calendar Accounts.

I'm not getting time to leave alerts

Allow notifications for Nudge in the Settings app, and make sure Time Sensitive notifications are allowed, including in any Focus you use. In Nudge, go to Settings › Notifications and tap Send a test alert to check they reach you. Alerts only come for events with a location, and can be turned off for a single event on its screen.

Leave times aren't updating when Nudge is closed

iOS decides how often apps can check in the background. For the most up-to-date times, set Location to Always and turn on Use location in the background in Settings › Notifications. Opening Nudge always shows the latest times.

An event has the wrong place, name or type

Use calendar rules: Settings › Calendars › Calendar rules. You can rename events, set or remove their location, choose a type, hide things like "Focus time", or turn off alerts. Nudge doesn't change the event in your calendar.

How do I sync Nudge between my devices?

Turn on Settings › iCloud sync on each device, signed in to the same Apple Account. Your settings, rules, types and places then stay the same everywhere. Your events are synced by your calendar provider, so turn on the same calendars on each device.

What's free and what's in Nudge Pro?

Leave times and alerts are always free. Nudge Pro adds Live Activities, the assistant, the morning summary, wake-up alarms, fine-tuned leave times, people features, and every theme, colour and app icon. It's £2.99 a month, or £20 a year with a 1-week free trial.

How do I restore Nudge Pro?

In Nudge, go to Settings › About and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the Apple Account you bought it with.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Open the Settings app › your name › Subscriptions › Nudge. You keep Pro until the end of the period you've paid for.
